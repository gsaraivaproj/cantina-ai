# Stock and orders

## Stock

Each product has a stock count. Every product sold takes one unit out of stock,
including the products a student picks inside a combo. A cancelled sale puts its units
back.

The cafeteria restocks every Monday morning, bringing each product back to its usual
level. Savory snacks and sweets come from the bakery supplier; drinks and other products
come from the distributor. Each stock entry records the quantity and the unit cost.

A product with no stock is not offered at the kiosk. When a product sells faster than
the weekly restock, it runs out before the next Monday.

## Combos

A combo lets the student pick one product from each of its categories (for example, one
savory snack and one drink) for a fixed price, lower than buying them separately.

## Orders

Every sale creates an order for the counter. In schools that use the two-step flow, the
order is placed first and handed over a few minutes later, when the snack is ready; the
counter marks it as delivered. In the other schools the order is delivered at once.

An order still waiting long after it was paid was probably handed over without being
marked, or the student never came to pick it up. The counter should check before marking
it as delivered or cancelling the sale.

# Balances and purchases

Every student has a prepaid balance. Guardians top it up, and the student spends it at
the counter or at a self-service kiosk.

## Top-ups

A top-up adds money to a student's balance. It is registered by an operator at the
counter, paid by PIX or in cash, and the money goes into that operator's open cash
register. Guardians usually top up when the balance gets low, in round amounts
(R$ 50, R$ 80, R$ 100, R$ 150).

## Buying with the balance

A purchase debits the student's balance by the total of its items. Prices come from
the active menu of the school; when the menu changes, new purchases use the new prices
and old purchases keep the price they were sold at.

The kiosk only completes a purchase when the balance covers it. At the counter, the
operator may complete it anyway, for example when a guardian promised a top-up the same
day. In that case the balance becomes negative, and the next top-up covers the debt
first.

## Paying in cash at the counter

A student without enough balance can pay for the purchase in cash on the spot. The
system records the purchase and, right after it, a cash payment linked to it. Neither
of them changes the balance: the purchase was paid with money, not with the balance.
The cash goes into the operator's register.

## Daily limit

Guardians can set a daily limit. When the school enables daily limits, the kiosk refuses
a purchase that would make the student spend more than the limit in the day, even if
the balance is enough. Lunch does not count against the limit.

## Lunch

Lunch has a variable price, typed by the operator according to the plate. It is charged
to the balance even when the balance is not enough: the school does not refuse a meal to
a student. This is the most common reason for a negative balance, and the guardian is
expected to top up afterwards.

## Cancelling a purchase

An operator can cancel a purchase shortly after it, for example when a product was not
available after all. The purchase is marked as cancelled and a refund gives the amount
back to the balance. The products return to stock and the order is cancelled.

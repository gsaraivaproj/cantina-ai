# Cash registers

Each operator works with their own cash register. A register opens with the first
movement of the day and is closed at the end of the day.

## What goes into a register

- Top-ups registered by the operator, by PIX or in cash.
- Cash payments of purchases made at the counter.
- Counter sales: purchases by teachers, staff and visitors, who have no student account.

PIX top-ups also count in the register's balance: the register reflects everything the
operator received, not only the physical cash.

## What goes out

- Withdrawals during the day, when there is too much cash in the drawer. The money goes
  to the school's safe.
- Refunds of cancelled counter sales.
- The end-of-day close: a withdrawal of everything left, right before the register is
  marked as closed. A closed register always ends with a balance of zero.

## Checking a register

A register is correct when its balance equals the sum of its movements: what came in
minus what went out. A register that is still open the next day was probably forgotten
by its operator and should be closed after the money in the drawer is counted.
